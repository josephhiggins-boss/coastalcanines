Training

Help for every stage of dog life

Whether it's training you need, somewhere to socialise your pup, or just space to play — Coastal Canines has you covered.

01

Secure Field Hire

A safe, closed-off area in our field where your pup can play lead-free to their heart's content. Perfect for zoomies, recall practice, nervous or reactive dogs — or just quality time together off the lead.

30 min – 1 hour, depending on their needs

02

1:1 Dog Training

Private sessions tailored entirely to you and your dog — obedience, recall, loose-lead walking, confidence building or behavioural training. You set the goals, we build the plan.

All ages & breeds welcome

03

Group Training Sessions

Learn alongside other local dogs and their humans. Real-world distractions, shared wins and a bit of craic — the skills stick better when practising around company.

Small, friendly groups

04

Dog Socialising

Gentle, structured socialising in a safe space — ideal for pups learning the ropes or older dogs who need calm, positive experiences around others. Kind, judgement-free, at your dog's pace.

Confidence first, always

About

The human behind the hounds

East Cork dog lovers, we have you covered. Coastal Canines is based right next to the sea in Ballycotton — a safe, spacious spot where local dogs come to train, socialise and simply run free.

Training here is always kind and reward-based. No shouting, no scary equipment, no "dominance" nonsense — just clear communication, good timing and plenty of treats. The goal isn't a robot dog; it's a happy one that listens because it wants to.
